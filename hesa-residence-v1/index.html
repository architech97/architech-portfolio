Arrive. Gather. Retreat.

The ground-floor plan brings an entry vestibule, living room and dining area together with a mandir and kitchen. The private program includes a master bedroom lounge, master bedroom, master toilet and dresser, alongside another bedroom, a toilet and a common toilet.

A quieter layer of home.

The first-floor layout is organised around a family lounge and vestibule, with bedrooms, toilets and terraces. The original numbered room key keeps each space easy to locate without changing the plan.

Explore the first floor
02

Space to switch off.

On the second floor, a home theatre and gaming zone introduce a distinct leisure program. A bedroom and toilet sit alongside the terrace areas, as shown in the furnished layout and technical drawing.

READING THE EXTERIOR

The supplied exterior renderings show pale façades, classical cornices, tall glazing, dark balcony railings and planted edges. Daylight reveals the proportions; the evening views reveal the warm illumination. These are observations from the visuals, not additional material specifications.
