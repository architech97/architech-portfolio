01 / 09 · From drawing to dwelling

It begins as a drawing.

The ground-floor plan, exactly as HESA Studio drew it. Black lines on paper — for now.

The drawing becomes the building.

This is the studio’s own 3D model, cut at 1.4 m — the height an architect cuts a plan. Sofas, beds and the dining table land on the lines that drew them.

Arrive. Gather.

Past the mandir and the vestibule, one continuous living and dining room — with the kitchen and a garden sit-out beyond.

Step inside.

Stand in the dining room and look up: the living room rises two storeys, 23 by 19 feet, open to the floor above.

Rise through the void.

Up past the family lounge to the first floor — three bedroom suites around a lounge that looks down into the living room.

Space to switch off.

Home theatre, gaming zone and a guest suite on the second floor. Then the roof closes the house and the garden grows in.

Three levels. One core.

The levels part. Stair and lift stack in exactly the same place on every floor — the spine of the house.

A day on the façade.

Street level, the south-west corner. Watch the sun cross the cornices, columns and garden. 07:00

And then, evening.

The wall lights and the rooms come on — the house glows rather than shines.
